Features

Everything you need to own the ocean

Toggle any cheat instantly with a hotkey — no hex editors, no memory scanners, no mod loaders.

🛡️

God Mode

Ignore leviathan bites, crush depth damage, radiation and thermal hazards. Your health and vehicle hull stay pinned so you can explore the deepest biomes without fear.

🫧

Infinite Oxygen

Never resurface again. Oxygen stays locked at maximum while diving, and the tank drain is cancelled for both the player and any piloted submersible.

🔨

Free Crafting

Build anything for zero resources. The fabricator and habitat builder skip material checks entirely, so you can lay out a full base in minutes instead of hours.

📍

Teleport

Jump to any saved coordinate, biome marker or custom waypoint on the map. Save your favorite wreck and return to it instantly on the next session.

📦

Item Spawner

Search the full item database and spawn resources, tools, fragments and upgrade modules straight into your inventory — with adjustable stack sizes.

⚡

Speed Hack

Adjust swim, walk and vehicle speed from 1× to 10× with a slider in the overlay. Great for grinding long traversal routes or escaping a bad encounter.
